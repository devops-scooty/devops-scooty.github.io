For Vendors & Partners

Becoming a vendor is quick and easy:

  1. Download the "Scooty XP Store" app from Google Play or App Store
  2. Click "Sign Up" and select "Store Owner" as your account type
  3. Fill in your business details (name, location, category, phone, email)
  4. Provide your bank account information for payouts
  5. Upload your business registration/tax documents
  6. Submit for verification (usually 24-48 hours)
  7. Once approved, customize your store and start adding products!

There is no setup fee. We charge a transparent ~9.1% commission per order.

We charge a transparent, per-order commission model:

  • Standard Commission: 9.1% of order subtotal
  • What's included: Platform access, customer reach, order processing, delivery logistics coordination, customer support
  • What you keep: 90.9% of your order value minus delivery costs (if applicable)
  • No hidden fees: No monthly subscription, no activation fee, no minimum order threshold

You set your own prices, and we deduct the commission only from orders placed through the Scooty XP platform. Your offline sales are 100% yours.

We offer flexible payout schedules:

  • Daily Payouts: Instant settlement to your bank account (available for select vendors)
  • Weekly Payouts: Every Friday for orders completed in the past 7 days
  • Manual Payout Request: Request settlement anytime (processed within 24 hours)
  • Minimum withdrawal: ₦1,000
  • No payout fees - money goes directly to your account

You can track all earnings and payouts in real-time via your Store Dashboard.

Every store gets a unique QR code that customers can scan to:

  • Visit your store directly on the Scooty XP app
  • See your menu, products, and prices
  • Place orders instantly

You can print this QR code and display it in your physical store, on flyers, business cards, or social media. This bridges offline-to-online sales perfectly. You can also download analytics showing how many scans and conversions your QR code generates.

Our Store App features non-destructive inventory management:

  • Set stock levels for each product
  • Mark items as "Out of Stock" without removing them from your menu
  • Stock is auto-updated when orders are placed
  • Get alerts when items are running low
  • Bulk edit inventory and pricing in seconds
  • Manage different versions of products (sizes, varieties)

This means you never lose your product history - you can easily reactivate items when you restock, keeping your store complete and organized.

We provide built-in marketing tools to help grow your sales:

  • QR codes for offline-to-online marketing
  • Store analytics (views, clicks, orders, conversion rates)
  • Promotional banners and deals section
  • Seasonal campaigns and featured products placement
  • Customer reviews and ratings system
  • Email campaigns to customers (coming soon)
  • Integration with your social media

Higher ratings and active promotion lead to better visibility on the platform, driving more customer orders.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Scooty, we want you to feel confident when ordering. This policy explains when you can cancel, how refunds work, and what to expect if something goes wrong.

1. When You Can Cancel

  • Orders can be cancelled directly in the Scooty app before the restaurant/vendor accepts them.
  • Once preparation or dispatch has started, cancellation may not be possible. If allowed, only a partial refund may apply.
  • For scheduled deliveries or bookings (e.g., events, travel), cancellations must be made within the specified notice period (usually 24–48 hours) to qualify for a refund.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be requested in the following situations:

  • Orders cancelled before preparation or dispatch.
  • Orders not delivered or delivered more than 30 minutes after ETA.
  • Orders with missing, incorrect, or damaged items.
  • Payments processed in error (e.g., duplicate charges).

3. Refund Destination

  • Refunds will be credited back to your original payment method (card, bank transfer, wallet).
  • If the original method is unavailable, Scooty will issue the refund to your Scooty Wallet, which can be used for future orders or withdrawn to a bank account upon request.
  • Instant Wallet Credits: For minor issues (e.g., missing side items), Scooty will issue immediate wallet credits while processing the full refund separately.

4. Automatic Compensation

  • For undelivered orders, Scooty will automatically issue a refund without requiring a claim.
  • For late deliveries, Scooty will apply the Late Delivery Compensation Framework (wallet credits + loyalty points).

5. How to Request a Refund or Cancel

  • Open the Scooty app → go to the Orders tab → select your order → tap Cancel Order.
  • For scheduled services, contact Scooty Support through the app.
  • Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the issue.
  • Supporting evidence (photos, order details, receipts) may be required for claims involving incorrect or damaged items.

6. Refund Timing

  • Requests are acknowledged within 24 hours.
  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

7. Important Notes

  • Promo codes and discounts are not refundable. However, Scooty may issue goodwill loyalty points if promo credits were applied to a failed order.
  • Refunds will not apply if delivery fails due to incorrect addresses or customer unavailability.
  • For travel/event bookings, tiered cancellation rules may apply (e.g., partial refunds if cancelled well in advance, no refunds close to the date).

8. Transparency & Goodwill

Scooty commits to handling refunds and cancellations fairly, promptly, and transparently. Customers affected by refund delays will receive bonus loyalty points as a goodwill gesture.
